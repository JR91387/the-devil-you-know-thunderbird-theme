**Name:** The Devil You Know

**Summary (≤ 250 characters):**
White panes, one blue accent, clear lines. For anyone who spent years in a big-office mail app and wants Thunderbird to feel familiar on day one. Light and dark.

**Description:**
The Devil You Know is a crisp light and dark theme for Thunderbird and Betterbird.

- Clean white panes with thin dividers instead of floating boxes.
- One blue accent: links, unread subjects, the New Message button, and a blue edge on the selected message.
- A matching dark mode that follows your system setting.
- Built on Thunderbird's own style variables, so anything the theme doesn't cover keeps Thunderbird's default look.

Tip: View → Layout → Vertical View gives the familiar three-column layout.

Free and open source (MIT). Source, wiki and screenshots: https://github.com/JR91387/the-devil-you-know-thunderbird-theme
